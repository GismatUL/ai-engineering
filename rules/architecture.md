# Architecture Rules

- Understand the existing architecture before making changes.
- Follow established project patterns where they are reasonable.
- Keep controllers thin.
- Keep business logic out of controllers.
- Prefer clear separation of responsibilities.
- Use dependency injection where appropriate.
- Do not introduce repositories, services, patterns, or abstractions without a clear need.
- Preserve existing business rules.
- Avoid changing architecture during a focused bug fix or performance optimization unless necessary.
- Prefer simple solutions over unnecessary architectural complexity.
